Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Is this TCPA compliant?
Yes. Before any campaign sends, you attest that the clients on your CSV have given prior express written consent to receive marketing messages from your business. Every message includes one-tap STOP opt-out, and we maintain suppression lists across campaigns. We're built around the FCC's 2025 One-to-One Consent Rule from day one.
How does iMessage delivery actually work?
When a client uses an iPhone, we deliver as iMessage — the blue-bubble experience clients trust and respond to. If iMessage isn't available, we fall back to RCS or standard SMS automatically. Your clients see a great experience either way; you don't have to think about it.
Who funds the rewards?
You do — we just make the math easy. You set the reward amount (typically $5–$25). We only deduct from your reward budget when a client actually completes the action. Unclaimed rewards stay in your account.
Will Google flag us for incentivizing reviews?
Our review flow is built to comply with Google's policy: rewards are conditioned on submitting a review (any star rating), never on submitting a positive one. The reward fires regardless of the score the client leaves. This is the same standard used by Podium, Birdeye, and other major review platforms.
What's the difference between the plans?
Starter is for solo practitioners — review campaigns only, 500 sends/month cap. Growth unlocks all three campaigns, unlimited sends, and priority support — perfect for single-location businesses. Pro adds multi-location management, lower per-result pricing ($2.50 vs $3), and dedicated account support for brands with multiple locations.
How long until I see results?
Most review campaigns send within 24 hours of upload and start producing reviews the same day. Reactivation and recapture campaigns are scheduled across the week to keep your reply volume manageable. Expect your first result within hours.
Do I need to integrate my CRM or booking software?
No. That's the point. Export a CSV from whatever system you already use, drag it in, and you're done. If you want deeper integrations later (live syncs, automated triggers off bookings), we offer those on the Pro plan — but they're optional.
